Definitions and how the numbers are made
Active user
Someone who opened Reacti on that day (daily), in the last 7 days (weekly) or 28 days (monthly).
Reacti
A photo or video message. Each one captures the friend’s reaction when opened.
Reaction captured
A reaction recorded and delivered back to the sender.
Stickiness
Average daily users ÷ monthly users (DAU/MAU).
Quick ratio
(New + returning users) ÷ users who stopped, over the same weeks.
Retention (Day N)
Rolling: of people who first opened the app at least N days ago, the share seen again N or more days later.
Benchmarks
Industry reference points from a16z, Amplitude and Sequoia write-ups on consumer apps. Useful direction, not targets.
Coverage
Counts people on app 1.6.0 or newer who keep “Usage data” on. Aggregates only: no individual, message, photo, video or face is ever collected for analytics.
Refresh
Rebuilt every hour from live production data; an open page reloads itself every 15 minutes.
